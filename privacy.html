G:AS Economy Bot Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the G:AS Economy Bot, currently operating as BrickBot ("the Bot"), collects, stores, and uses information when providing its Discord economy features.

Information We Collect

The Bot stores only information necessary to operate its economy and related features. This may include:

  • Discord server IDs
  • Discord user IDs
  • Economy balances ("Bricks")
  • Inventory contents and item quantities
  • Command cooldown timestamps
  • Server-specific Bot settings and economy configuration

The Bot does not intentionally store message contents, passwords, Discord account credentials, email addresses, IP addresses, payment information, or other sensitive personal information.

How Information Is Used

Stored information is used solely to provide and maintain the Bot's functionality, including tracking balances, inventories, cooldowns, server-specific economy settings, and other economy features.

Information collected by the Bot is not sold, used for advertising, or used to profile users.

Data Sharing

Data stored by the Bot is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising, marketing, or data-brokering purposes.

The Bot interacts with Discord and therefore processes information obtained through Discord as necessary to provide its functionality.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Bot and its economy features. Data may be removed when it is no longer necessary for the Bot's operation, when the Bot stops operating, or when deletion is requested as described below.

Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of data associated with their Discord account by contacting the Bot administrator:

Somniputt/Rose

Please provide enough information to identify the Discord account whose Bot data should be deleted. Additional verification may be requested when necessary to ensure that a deletion request is being made by the appropriate user.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information stored by the Bot from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Bot's functionality or data practices change. The latest version of this policy will be made available at this page.

Discord

The Bot operates using Discord's platform and API. Use of Discord itself is subject to Discord's own terms and privacy policies. This Privacy Policy applies only to data processed by the G:AS Economy Bot.
